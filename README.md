# Costcina

A costing and back office tool for restaurant owners. It makes it easy to calculate prime cost on every dish, engineer the menu to be profitable, and inform the strategy to increase plate spend.

Site: https://costcina.com
Demo: https://demo.costcina.com (fictional food truck, made-up data)

## The problem

Owners don't know their real plate costs.

## What it does

- Costs every dish from its ingredients, including sub-recipes used inside other recipes.
- Keeps food, labor and packaging costs separate, so each dish shows its prime cost.
- Updates every dish when an ingredient price changes.
- Works in English and Spanish.
